EXTENSIÓN SECUNDARIA / SECONDARY EXTENSION
animal_indicators_2013.csv stores each count, both denominators and both ratios.
Heads/all UPAs is not herd size among owners. Species-owning municipal UPA counts
are unavailable in the selected aggregate. Family members8+ are not used because
their producer universe is narrower than the animal numerator.
P_est2013 = P2012 + (P2024-P2012)/12, unrounded before rates. This is an estimated
census-year convention, not an official projection or observed2013 population.
Zero/nonpositive denominator -> missing rate with explicit cause. Counts remain
observed, while geographic and estimated-denominator limitations remain visible.

EXPLORACIÓN / EXPLORATION
catalogo_plantas_y_otras_variables.csv inventories municipal and Redatam fields,
plus the crop workbook. crop_source_inventory.csv counts records by crop code,
label and campaign; these are NOT numbers of plants or UPAs. Exploration report
distinguishes hectares, quintales, scattered fruit-tree counts and medicinal
species presence. No crop production or plant-stock indicator was manufactured.
Observed CM/CP/OT cultivation types need fuller definitions; campaign25 is not
observed separately. Crop/land areas must not be treated as counts of plants.
The Peru comparison is conceptual: the inspected source covers population and
housing, not agricultural stocks. No bilateral agricultural equivalence claimed.
